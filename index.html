An executive dashboard that gives a project management office one view of portfolio health. It is built on a three-table governance dataset covering 55 projects, 418 risks, and 497 change requests.

  • Portfolio status: total projects, completion rate, delayed projects, and projects by current status.
  • Budget control: planned budget against actual cost over time, budget utilization, and the projects with the largest variance.
  • Change requests: volume by change type and by approval status.
  • Risk management: total risks, risk closure rate, average risk probability, and risks by severity and mitigation status.
  • Interactive filters: slice every page by project and by strategic objective.
